My thoughts

A few ideas I keep coming back to.

01

Complex ideas deserve simple words

Whether it’s a stock market lesson or a model architecture, the real test of understanding is whether you can explain it to a beginner.

02

Stories travel further than facts

Films and books carry an idea into places a technical document never reaches. Good engineering benefits from the same craft.

03

Build in the open

Tools, weights and knowledge move faster when they are shared. Open source turns one person’s fix into everyone’s progress.
